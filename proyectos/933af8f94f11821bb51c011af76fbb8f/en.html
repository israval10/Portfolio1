CONTEXT:

The country's nutritional health situation concerns a number of population sectors.

According to the Health and Family Health Survey (Endes 2023), 40% of Peruvians older than 15 are overweight and 20% have obesity. That means that about 60% of the population in the country have a weight that isn't considered suitable for their age (Ministry of Health, 2024).

At this juncture Fimmashi adds up as a useful and amusing digital solution that aims to support the efforts of users committed to improving their well-being and challenges to this goal.

8 out of 10 Peruvians wish to have a healthy diet. Nevertheless, only 34% incorporates this into their daily routine and 45% do so occasionally. According to Impresa Research, his study Health Consumption Trends (2023).

PROBLEM

Patients who start a nutritional regimen often leave the plan established by their nutritionists, as the process of integrating nutritional habits into their daily activities ends up being overwhelming. The absence of a system with continuous monitoring and the necessary continuous motivation lead to their commitment to be diluted with the passing of the days.

SOLUTION

Fit Mashi is a nutritional counselling service designed to facilitate the formation of healthy eating habits in patients and also with them to solve daily difficulties that arise during this process. Thus its scope includes both face-to-face (nutritional) and digital (mobile) models.

The initiative offers custom nutritional schemes for people who wish to improve their general well-being and those who seek to achieve specific objectives. Fit Mashi's differential value lies in its approach to creating sustainable habits, supported by a gamification system that integrates goals, challenges and bonuses to maintain patient motivation throughout the process.

METHODS

To put into place the research process I chose to divide it into an exploratory and another conclusive phase, with this I would make sure that this will be as comprehensive as possible and that in turn I would be capable of providing substantiated recommendations to help build the service experience.

Stage 1 (Exploratory): Preliminary analysis

That instance, of an exploratory nature, allowed me to deeply explore the problem or user's need as well as understand the wider context in which it develops. The methods used at this stage have established a solid basis of initial understanding as well as collection of preliminary insights, identification of areas of interest and establishment of first hypotheses.

Stage 2 (C)oncluent): Survey analysis

At this concluding stage of the study, I chose to use polls as the main tool. That approach was useful to validate initial scenarios and complete understanding of users' preferences and needs on a large scale and to achieve metrics about satisfaction with current or potential experience.

A total of 124 surveys were conducted with individuals interested in improving their health habits. That sample covered an extensive age range from age 20 to people from age 40 and beyond, enabling a diversity of public representation.
